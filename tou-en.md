## 6. Accuracy and limitations

6.1 The Quranic text follows the verified Uthmani standard of the Madani Mushaf (Hafs ʿan ʿĀṣim, King Fahd Glorious Quran Printing Complex). If you notice an error in the text, please report it immediately to quranmuallem@gmail.com.

6.2 **Speech recognition is algorithmic.** Matching your recitation against the text is automatic and is provided "as is". It can mark correct recitation as wrong or miss mistakes, especially with background noise, a fast recitation pace or detailed Tajweed rules. The Quran phoneme engine compares phonemes (including madd, ghunna and shadda lengths); this automatic tajweed feedback can be wrong and does not replace a qualified teacher. It does not evaluate makharij in detail or melody, and it does not replace correction by a teacher.

6.3 The Qibla direction is calculated from your device's location and compass sensors. Magnetic interference, an uncalibrated sensor or an inaccurate location can lead to deviations. When in doubt, check the direction another way.

6.4 Recitation audio, Quran text from external services and other third-party content are available only while those services are available.

6.5 **Prayer times** are calculated with the method you chose (or provided by Aladhan for a city) and can differ by a few minutes from your local mosque, especially at high latitudes. Adhan notifications depend on your device: battery saving, "Do not disturb", silent mode or missing permissions can delay or suppress them. When in doubt, follow the times of your local mosque.
